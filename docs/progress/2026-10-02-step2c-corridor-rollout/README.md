# Corridor rollout

The owner's playtest note: *"roll out to the other hallways, I can't tell what the corridor is or where it is."* Their run had no Hallway room (only a Back Hall, still an ordinary room then), so the prototype never appeared. The owner also chose to fill in dead-end arms.

**What changed**
- All three corridor templates are corridors now: Hallway, Back Hall and Attic Landing.
- An arm that ends at an outside wall is filled in (`Logic/Corridor`), so a corridor becomes a straight, L or T hallway instead of a plus with alcoves. An arm stays open when it leads to another room (a doorway, or a solid wall where a phantom doorway can appear), or when it holds a window, a hiding spot or a Lantern room's shrine. Anything the template put in a filled arm is left out.
- The Back Hall lost its locker (no room for one in a corridor) and its floor lamp: corridors use one ceiling light, as ART.md describes. The Attic Landing keeps its wardrobe, and its window now closes the end of the south arm, so when that arm faces outside the corridor ends in moonlight.
- Across 1,000 generated houses, 96% have at least one corridor (2.7 on average), with 1.3 arms filled per corridor.

**Stalker test, tier 2.**
- About 400 position samples across the house: The Guest was never inside a closet or a filled arm.
- With the player standing in the Hallway's junction facing its south doorway, The Guest waited on the centre line of the open south arm, 12 to 19 studs out, just out of sight, as the observation rule says. Closest approach 8 studs.
- No errors in the console.
